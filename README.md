Project Overview

The Pirate Intelligent Agent project focused on applying reinforcement learning and neural networks to develop an intelligent agent capable of navigating a maze to locate treasure. The objective was to train an artificial intelligence (AI) agent to make decisions based on its environment, learn from previous experiences, and identify an effective path toward its goal.

The project provided an opportunity to explore how machine learning algorithms can solve problems that require sequential decision-making. Through this assignment, I gained a deeper understanding of how reinforcement learning can be implemented in Python and how an intelligent agent improves its decision-making through repeated interactions with an environment.

What Code Was Provided, and What Code Did I Create?
For this project, I was provided with a partially completed Jupyter Notebook containing the foundational components of the pirate treasure hunt environment. The starter code included the maze environment, supporting Python libraries, and portions of the reinforcement learning framework. These components established the environment in which the pirate agent would operate and provided the structure necessary to develop and evaluate its learning process.

My responsibility was to work with the provided framework to implement the deep Q-learning training process. This involved developing and troubleshooting the training logic that allowed the agent to interact with the maze, select actions, receive rewards, and learn from previous experiences. I worked with concepts such as experience replay, exploration versus exploitation, neural network predictions, and reward-based learning.

Throughout the project, I also encountered technical challenges within the Codio environment. Troubleshooting these issues reinforced the importance of debugging, testing, and understanding how individual components interact within a machine learning application.

What Do Computer Scientists Do, and Why Does It Matter?

Computer scientists analyze problems, design computational solutions, and develop technologies that improve how individuals and organizations process information and accomplish tasks. Their work extends beyond writing code to include algorithm development, system architecture, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and the ethical application of technology.

Computer science matters because modern society depends on reliable, secure, and efficient computing systems. From protecting sensitive information to improving healthcare technologies and automating complex processes, computer scientists help address challenges that affect individuals, businesses, and communities.

Throughout CS 370, I developed a greater appreciation for the relationship between computer science and artificial intelligence. Neural networks and reinforcement learning demonstrate how computational systems can recognize patterns, adapt to changing conditions, and support decision-making. As someone pursuing a career in information security, I recognize how these technologies can also contribute to threat detection, network monitoring, and the identification of unusual system behavior.

How Do I Approach a Problem as a Computer Scientist?

My approach to problem-solving begins with understanding the problem, identifying its requirements, and breaking it into smaller, manageable components. I consider the available information, evaluate possible solutions, and determine which algorithms or technologies are appropriate for the situation.

The Pirate Intelligent Agent project reinforced the importance of this structured approach. Developing an intelligent agent required understanding the maze environment, identifying possible actions, establishing a reward system, and evaluating how the agent could improve its decisions through experience.

I also learned that successful problem-solving requires patience, experimentation, and adaptability. When code does not execute as expected, it is important to examine error messages, isolate potential causes, and test changes systematically rather than making assumptions.

This course strengthened my ability to approach unfamiliar technical challenges analytically. It also demonstrated that computer science frequently involves iterative improvement rather than finding a perfect solution on the first attempt.

What Are My Ethical Responsibilities to the End User and the Organization?

As a computer scientist, I have an ethical responsibility to develop technologies that are secure, reliable, transparent, and respectful of the people who use them. This includes protecting sensitive information, reducing the potential for algorithmic bias, and ensuring that technological solutions do not create unnecessary risks for individuals or organizations.

Artificial intelligence introduces additional ethical considerations because automated systems can make decisions that affect users without direct human involvement. Developers must consider how training data, reward structures, and algorithm design influence system behavior. An AI system that performs well under controlled conditions may still produce unexpected or harmful results when exposed to unfamiliar situations.

Organizations also depend on computer scientists to protect their systems, maintain data integrity, and communicate technological limitations accurately. Ethical development requires thorough testing, responsible documentation, and accountability when problems arise.

My coursework has reinforced the importance of incorporating ethical considerations throughout the development process rather than treating them as an afterthought. As I pursue a career in cybersecurity, I intend to apply these principles by prioritizing security, user privacy, responsible automation, and continuous evaluation of the technologies I help develop.

Final Reflection

The Pirate Intelligent Agent project brought together several important concepts from CS 370, including neural networks, deep Q-learning, reinforcement learning, and intelligent decision-making. It helped me better understand how artificial intelligence systems can learn from experience rather than relying entirely on explicitly programmed instructions.

Although I encountered technical challenges during development, the experience strengthened my troubleshooting skills and demonstrated the importance of persistence and systematic problem-solving.

Most importantly, this course expanded my understanding of how artificial intelligence connects to the broader field of computer science. The concepts I explored will be valuable as I continue developing my technical skills and preparing for a career in information security.
